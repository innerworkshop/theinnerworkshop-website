Emotional Freedom Techniques (EFT)

Emotional Freedom Techniques (EFT) is considered an emerging therapeutic approach. While it is gaining scientific support and is increasingly accepted as a legitimate psychological technique, it is not yet universally recognised as a formally validated clinical intervention. Results may vary from person to person and no guarantees are made regarding individual outcomes.

Rapid Transformational Therapy (RTT®)

RTT® is a hybrid therapy that draws on principles from hypnotherapy, neuro-linguistic programming (NLP), cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) and psychotherapy. While many clients report significant and lasting benefits, individual responses vary and outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Reiki & Guided Visualisation

Reiki and guided visualisation are complementary wellness practices used for relaxation and stress reduction. They are not medical treatments and do not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. They are intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical or psychological care.
