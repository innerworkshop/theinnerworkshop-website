New Book

Tap, Tap, Little Worry

Meet Your Nine Friends

Chloe is worried about a test. Eddie is worried about the dark at night. Then nine woodland friends arrive — a woodpecker, an owl, a badger, a dormouse, a rabbit, a fox, a robin, a hedgehog and a deer — each with a gentle tapping spot and a kind word to help.

Tap along with the animals and discover a simple, calming tool children can use anywhere, any time. A rhyming story for ages 4 to 11, with notes for parents and teachers.

✎ Written by Sarah Cox 🎨 Illustrated by Mahnoor 📖 Ages 4–11 📚 Published by The Inner Workshop
