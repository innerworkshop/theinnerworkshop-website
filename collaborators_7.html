My Network

Collaborators & Trusted Professionals

The people I work alongside, refer to, and collaborate with. Each one someone I know, trust and believe in wholeheartedly. If what you or your child needs sits beyond the scope of my work, these are the first people I would point you towards.

Lisa Low

Beyond Bridges Education

Visit Website

Lisa is an international thought leader, consultant educator and speaker with over 30 years of experience spanning classrooms, communities and organisations across the globe. Her work is rooted in the neuroscience of relationships, regulation and belonging, helping educators, leaders and schools to see beyond labels and understand the behaviour they see in front of them.

Through her Beyond Bridges Education programmes, Lisa trains educators and practitioners in Emotion Coaching, giving them the tools to create genuinely inclusive, compassionate environments where every child can thrive.

If you are looking to deepen your understanding of emotion coaching or take your professional practice further, Lisa is the person I would send you to without hesitation.

Specialisms

Emotion Coaching · Neurodiversity · School and Educator Training · Inclusive Education · Keynotes and Workshops

Rachel Stacey-McKay

EFT Master Trainer and Supervisor

Visit Website

Rachel is an EFT Master Trainer and Supervisor with the EFT and Mindfulness Centre and a qualified teacher (BA hons, PGCE QTS). She is the person who trained me in EFT and I can say from personal experience that her teaching is exceptional.

Rachel's real passion lies in training and supervising other practitioners and educators in EFT, bringing this powerful tool into schools, workplaces and professional practice. If you are a teacher, school leader or professional who has experienced EFT and wants to learn how to use it yourself in your work, Rachel is the natural next step.

She can take you from initial awareness all the way through to full EFT Practitioner qualification, giving you the skills to support the children, young people or colleagues in your own professional setting.

Specialisms

EFT Master Trainer and Supervisor · Matrix Reimprinting · EFT Practitioner Training · Schools and Educators · Qualified Teacher

Lucy Cawkwell

Osborne Cawkwell Tuition

Visit Website

Osborne Cawkwell is one of London's most respected private tuition agencies, with over 25 years of experience matching families with hand-selected, carefully vetted tutors across all ages and subjects, from primary level through to A Level and beyond.

I worked as a consultant with Lucy Cawkwell for over 20 years and know that what sets OC apart is their genuinely child-centred, personalised approach. They work with a wide range of needs including SEND support, school entrance preparation and mental health-aware tuition, making them an excellent complement to the therapeutic and emotional wellbeing work I do.

If your child needs academic support alongside their wellbeing work, I would wholeheartedly recommend speaking with the team at Osborne Cawkwell.

Specialisms

Private Tuition · SEND Support · School Entrance Preparation · London and Online · Primary to A Level

Dan Licence

Education Mental Health Practitioner

LinkedIn Profile
danlicence@hotmail.co.uk

Dan is a qualified Education Mental Health Practitioner (PGDip, UCL) with over 10 years of experience supporting children and young people's mental wellbeing across the NHS, leading mental health charities including Mind and Rethink, and the education sector.

Dan has a particular specialism in working with boys aged 12+ and young men, helping them develop healthier ways of understanding and managing their emotions in a safe, non-judgmental space. His approach is grounded in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), exploring the relationship between thoughts, feelings and behaviours and building practical strategies for lasting change.

He works mainly online, making his support accessible wherever you are. With experience spanning the NHS, MHFA England, Mind, Rethink and Osborne Cawkwell, Dan brings exceptional breadth and credibility to his practice. If you have a son or young man in your life who needs support, Dan is someone I trust completely.

Specialisms

Boys and Young Men (12+) · CBT · Anxiety and Low Mood · Online Sessions · Schools and Organisations · Mental Health Training
